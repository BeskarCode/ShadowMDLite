Sheet 1 of 2 · FOOT controller · LEFT Joy-Con

Drives the droid and triggers dome panels, lights and sounds. Hold the Joy-Con upright, stick at the top, rail against your palm.

Driving. The stick drives. Holding L or ZL stops the motors, which is what lets you press combinations safely.
Stick off / on: stick click + Minus / stick click + Capture.
Over speed toggle: stick click + ZL.
Dome from the foot controller: hold ZL and use the stick.

Sheet 2 of 2 · DOME controller · RIGHT Joy-Con

Rotates the dome and works the body: utility arms, tools and doors. The four face buttons take the place of the d-pad, in the diamond you see with the Joy-Con upright.

—SL and SRnot used—
The dome stick rotates the dome.
Combinations written as dome + foot need both controllers: hold the button on this one and press the modifier on the foot Joy-Con. That is how Shadow has always worked, and it is what gives you more functions than one controller has buttons.
